Each step of the workflow has a risk, and each risk has a guardrail. These are the weak spots I found in my own plan and how each is handled. Customers prefer a human (AnswerConnect/OnePoll: 85%, 87% for local services), so the owner makes every call and AI voice is a later opt-in only.
